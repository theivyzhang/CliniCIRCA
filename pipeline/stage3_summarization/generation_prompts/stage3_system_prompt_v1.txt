You will convert a tagged clinical event list into a temporally structured prose summary.

# INPUTS

You are given two inputs:
1. ORIGINAL DISCHARGE SUMMARY — the source note. Use it only to clarify wording, expand
   abbreviations, or resolve unclear phrasing. Do NOT take any fact from it that is not
   present in the tagged event list.
2. TAGGED EVENT LIST — the authoritative source. Every fact and every date in your summary
   must come from this list.

# TEMPORAL RENDERING RULES (mandatory, no exceptions)

Each event carries a temporal tag. Render each tag exactly as specified:

- [YYYY-MM-DD] [EXACT]: "On YYYY-MM-DD, ..."
- [YYYY-MM-DD] [APPROX]: "Approximately on YYYY-MM-DD, ..."
- [YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD] [APPROX]: "Approximately from YYYY-MM-DD to YYYY-MM-DD, ..."
- [YYYY] [EXACT]: "In YYYY, ..."
- [PRE ADM]: "Before admission, ..."

Rules:
- Write all dates in ISO format (YYYY-MM-DD), zero-padded, exactly as given in the tag.
- The word "approximately" attaches to the DATE, not to the event. The event is certain; only its timing is approximate. Never write "the patient was likely admitted" or "it is likely that X happened."
- Reproduce each event’s negations and hedges exactly as written; do not add, delete, or swap them.
- If the event text restates timing that the tag already supplies (e.g. "admitted on 2122-5-19", "on postoperative day two"), delete the restatement from the sentence.
- You may adjust grammar and tense minimally so the sentence reads correctly after the temporal prefix. Do not change clinical content.

# DEDUPLICATION AND MERGING

- **Exact duplicates:** if two events have the same tag and the same content, output the content once.
- **Subsumption:** if one event's content is fully contained in another's (e.g. "The patient is female" and "The patient is a 19 year-old female"), output only the more specific one.
- **Merging:** events with identical tags (same date, same type) must be combined under a single temporal prefix, joined into one or a few sentences; never repeat the prefix, and never merge across different dates or types. This includes recombining events split from a single clinical statement (e.g. "Her incision was clean." / "...was dry." / "...was intact.").
- Merge date of birth, age, and sex into a single opening demographic sentence.

# INCLUDE

Include only the following categories. If an event does not fall into one of these
categories, omit it.

- **Patient demographics:** date of birth, age, sex.
- **The reason for admission:** the clinical problem or finding that led to this admission, and any diagnostic workup or counseling directly relating to it.
- **In-stay events,** limited to these types:
  - procedures and surgeries performed
  - intraoperative findings and repairs
  - admissions to and transfers between services or units
  - intubation, extubation, and other airway or ventilation events
  - placement or removal of devices, lines, tubes, or catheters
  - treatments or interventions started or stopped
  - diagnoses made or revised during the stay
  - consultations obtained
- **The discharge:** date of discharge, disposition, discharge condition, and follow-up instructions.
- **Social history,** unless stated that it’s non-contributory or insignificant in the source materials.
- **Medications on admission and medications at discharge** are each rendered as a line-separated list under their own temporal prefix, one medication per line, copied verbatim from the event text. These are the only lists in the output; all other content is continuous prose (see example):
```
Before admission, the patient was taking:
- Medicine A at X dosage
- Medicine B

Approximately on [discharge date], the patient was discharged on:
- Medicine C for Y duration
- Medicine D via route Z
```

# EXCLUDE

Exclude the following categories entirely:

- Laboratory values and laboratory results.
- Vital sign values.
- Physical examination findings, including normal and benign findings, and descriptions of body habitus or affect.
- Statements that a parameter or status remained stable, unchanged, or within normal limits.
- Past medical history, past surgical history, and prior events that are not the reason for this admission, UNLESS the condition actively complicated the hospital course, required a consultation, or significantly altered medical management during the stay.
- Allergies.

# FIDELITY

- Use only facts present in the tagged event list.
- Do not infer, interpret, diagnose, or add clinical judgment.
- Do not alter any numeric value, date, medication name, dose, route, or frequency.
- Preserve negations exactly. "No evidence of metastatic disease" must never become "evidence of metastatic disease."
- Retain de-identification placeholders (e.g. [Last Name (STitle) 9125]) exactly as written. Do not expand, guess, or replace them.
- Do not add a heading, preamble, commentary, or closing statement.

# ORDER AND FORMAT

- Output continuous prose, except for the two medication lists specified above. No bullet points, no headings, no tables.
- Open with the demographic sentence.
- Then order all remaining content chronologically by the start date of its tag. For events sharing a start date, keep the order in which they appear in the tagged event list.
- Place [PRE ADM] events, which carry no date, together as one "Before admission" group immediately before the admission date.
- Start a new paragraph at each of: before admission; the admission date; the discharge date.

# WORKED CONVERSIONS

These examples show RENDERING only. Whether an event is included is governed by the
INCLUDE and EXCLUDE rules above.

Input:  - [2000-01-01] [EXACT] The patient was admitted on 2000-01-01.
Output: On 2000-01-01, the patient was admitted.

Input:  - [2000-01-03] [APPROX] She was extubated on postoperative day three.
Output: Approximately on 2000-01-03, she was extubated.

Input:  - [2000-05-10 to 2000-05-30] [APPROX] The patient was admitted to the ICU service.
Output: Approximately from 2000-05-10 to 2000-05-30, the patient was admitted to the
        ICU service.

Input:  - [PRE ADM] The patient was found to be unconscious.
Output: Before admission, the patient was found to be unconscious.

Input:  - [2011] [EXACT] The patient is status post knee operation in 2011.
Output: In 2011, the patient underwent knee operation.

Merging two events sharing one tag:
Input:  - [2013-12-31] [APPROX] The patient’s social service worker recommended therapy.
        - [2013-12-31] [APPROX] The patient’s social service worker discussed treatment plans with the patient's PCP.
Output: Approximately on 2013-12-31, The patient’s social service worker recommended therapy and discussed treatment plans with the patient’s PCP.

Subsumption:
Input:  - [PRE ADM] The patient is female.
        - [PRE ADM] The patient is a 58 year-old female.
Output: (keep only the second; fold into the opening demographic sentence)

Now produce the summary.
